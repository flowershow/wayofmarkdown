# Reference review, 2026-10-02

Rufus's verdicts on the candidate gallery (https://claude.ai/artifact/3yFuLuEBGzVquzbyCS2MLJ), lightly tidied, his words kept. Candidates list: `candidates-2026-10-02.md`. The same sites are logged in `~/src/rufuspollock/ux-patterns/docs-and-explainer-sites/`.

## Yes, or useful

- **Devouring Details** (https://devouringdetails.com/): "yes!" The most obvious significant alternative to our blue direction. Likes the orange: much warmer than our blue, could be the primary emphasis (text links), or paired with blue, though readability may struggle.
- **Maggie Appleton** (https://maggieappleton.com/): a definite possibility. Quite different: more rounded and full, garden-like. The Wikimedia-style image could be used more there.
- **Sketchplanations** (https://sketchplanations.com/): interesting font and feel. A contender for a third direction: quite different in font, spacing, colour, illustration style.
- **Apple Human Interface Guidelines** (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines): Apple design is always beautiful. Really nice colours and use of their shading. Don't necessarily copy.
- **Ink & Switch** (https://www.inkandswitch.com/): "love it, cool design." More a research-site register, so not for this site, but a useful possible direction to keep in mind.
- **Stripe Press** (https://press.stripe.com/): really lovely, interesting. More for a catalogue than for us; could borrow ideas for a catalogue of markdown apps or of recommended reading. Not sure it's worth it, but cool.
- **Teenage Engineering** (https://teenage.engineering/): the home page is more interesting than the guides, super punk. Not us, maybe worth referencing.
- **Andy Matuschak** (https://andymatuschak.org/ more than the notes): yes, but not different enough from our blue site. Fun font. A good also-ran, useful for other things, e.g. a notebook site.
- **Distill** (https://distill.pub/): good for reports. Maybe for the layout of some of our pages, not for the site.
- **US Graphics / Berkeley Mono** (https://usgraphics.com/products/berkeley-mono): cool, a reference for datapressr rather than here (bead filed).

## No, for this site

- **Stripe docs**: OK, but he'd choose what we have over it. Adds nothing.
- **Linear docs**: very elegant, but not much versus Co-Star, which has more personality.
- **Raycast manual**: meh.
- **Tailwind docs**: great, nothing for us.
- **Josh Comeau**: boring for us.
- **The Pudding**: not for us.
- **Byrne's Euclid**: no. Old-school font gets in the way, too much, not elegant.

## What this means for the visual round

The three feels are now: 1 the manual, warmed (built, option 1); 2 the warm alternative, from Devouring Details (paper, orange as emphasis, worked examples); 3 the illustrated one, from Maggie Appleton and Sketchplanations (rounded, garden-like, drawing-led). The earlier "plain text / code.storage" feel drops out of the three unless Rufus asks for it back; code.storage stays a reference for how a viz explains.
