> I wouldn't say it's the worst format one. I don't like that phrasing. It's poor. It's more about how the tools were often worse over time. It's not that Markdown was a format compared to note. It's that you're comparing absolutes.
>
> The story of this thing is the story of this way. Why it's called the way of Markdown is that it's more about the ecosystem of tooling built around it, particularly a more open-source ecosystem of tooling that took over.
